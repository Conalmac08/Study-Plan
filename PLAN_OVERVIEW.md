# Week-by-week plan – snapshot (3 Oct 2026 → end of the June 2027 exams)

This is a **printable snapshot** of what the app's planner produces with the default settings: Saturday work day, Mon 3.5 h · Tue 2 h + Maths grind · Wed 4.5 h · Thu 3.5 h · Fri 3 h · Sunday 2 h, 50-minute blocks, and holidays at 4.5 h a day.

**The live plan in the app is the one to follow.** It re-plans every day from your topic ratings, the dates you edit, coursework hours left and your settings. Hours are focused study time only (breaks aren't counted) and include coursework blocks and the Tuesday grind. Exam dates after April are predictions until the SEC publishes the timetable. Mocks: no changes, as you asked.

How each stretch works:
- **Coursework first:** each milestone gets its blocks spread evenly up to the day before it's due.
- **DCG SolidWorks parts stay in school.** The app shows them as "In school today" reminders on DCG class days, never as homework.
- **Everything else** is split between subjects by grade gap × marks still in the written exam. Within each subject it follows the marks each section carries, then topic frequency, difficulty, your confidence and spaced repetition.
- **Same-day recap:** on school days the first block reviews a subject you had in class that day.
- **Irish oral:** about 1 block a week until Christmas, then about 2 a week in January, 3 in February and daily in March until your oral.
- **From 5 April:** most blocks are timed SEC past-paper sections. During the exams each evening goes to the next paper.
