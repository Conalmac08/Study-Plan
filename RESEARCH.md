## 3. Subject findings

**Physics – new specification (first examined 2027).**
- Strands: Unifying Strand (Nature of Science); 1 Forces & Motion; 2 Wave Motion & Energy Transfer; 3 Electric & Magnetic Fields; 4 Modern Physics (Atomic & Nuclear).
- Confirmed element headings:
  - Strand 1: 1.1–1.6 (straight-line motion, forces, stretching & compressing, work–energy, gravitational field, uniform circular motion).
  - Strand 3: 3.1 Charge interactions, 3.2 Modelling electric fields, 3.3 Electric circuits, 3.4 Magnetic fields, plus induced pd / generator effect.
  - Strand 4: 4.1 The electron, 4.2 Photoelectric emission & X-rays, 4.3 Early models of the atom, 4.4 Radioactivity, 4.5 Mass–energy equivalence, 4.6 Harnessing nuclear energy.
- ⚠ Strand 2's numbering and the exact learning-outcome wording couldn't be opened – check the spec PDF.
- Exam (SEC sample papers, via StudyClix): 2 h 30 min. Q1 is compulsory, then answer 5 of the other 6; all questions are 50 marks. The SEC has published two Higher Level sample papers.
- Your 'learn' topics are marked and scheduled for the week they're likely to be taught (estimates). Tick **Taught** in the app when your class covers one.
- Physics in Practice Investigation: 200 marks, common level, SEC deadline Fri 11 Dec 2026. ⚠ The 2027 brief's theme is reported as domestic sustainability (energy sources, usage or losses), which fits your bulb/heat question. ⚠ StudyClix summarises it as about 50 marks per area with a report of about 1,500 words – check your copy of the brief.

**English HL** (Circular 0021/2025).
- 2027 HL comparative modes: **Theme or Issue · Cultural Context · General Vision and Viewpoint**.
- 2027 HL poets: **Bishop, Dickinson, Donne, Kavanagh, Mahon, Meehan, Rich, Yeats**. All five of yours are on it, and the full poem lists for your five are in the app.
- *Othello* is your Shakespeare single text. *1984*, *Small Things Like These* and *The Shawshank Redemption* are all on the 2027 comparative list.
- At least four poets always appear, so with five studied at least one of yours must come up. Still get four essay-ready.

**Irish OL** (Circular 0041/2026 – prescribed material for 2027 and 2028, which reflects SEC circular S76/25 on the 2027/28 orals).
- Your poems are confirmed with these exact titles: **"Dínit an Bhróin"** (Máirtín Ó Direáin), **"Iníon"** (Áine Durkin), **"Glaoch Abhaile"** (Áine Ní Ghlinn).
- The other prescribed poems are "Deireadh na Feide" (Ailbhe Ní Ghearbhuigh) and "Úirchill an Chreagáin" (Art Mac Cumhaigh).
- ⚠ Prose (from secondary sources): Clann Lir, Cuairteoir, An tIriseoir, and option groups (Eoinín na nÉan / An Féileacán agus an Crann Úll; Athair / Glantóir / An Bóthar go Santiago). Confirm your class's choices.
- Oral (240 marks): fáiltiú 5, filíocht 35, sraith pictiúr 80, comhrá 120. Comhrá plus sraith is 200 of the 240.
- A 70% oral (168 of 600) means an O6 (240) needs only about 20% of the written marks.
- ⚠ One article says the oral will drop to 25% "in coming years". The SEC's 2027 arrangements still show the 240-mark oral, so the plan treats it as 40%.

**Maths HL.** All five strands, with calculus weighted ×1.6 because it's your weakest area. The Tuesday grind counts as one Maths block.

**Geography HL.** 2027 is the **last year of the current syllabus**; the new specification is first examined in 2028. The core is covered in full. Your elective (economic / human) and option (Global Interdependence / Geoecology / Culture & Identity / Atmosphere–Ocean) are switchable placeholders in Settings.

**DCG HL.**
- Written paper: 3 h, 240 marks. Section A is 3 of 4 short questions; Section B is 2 of 3 long questions; Section C is the applied graphics options.
- Your kettle brief details (≤ 10 A3 pages, Outputs 1–7, 5–10 SolidWorks parts, USB structure, freehand sketches for Outputs 2 & 6) come from you ⚠. I couldn't open the 2027 SEC brief.

**Technology HL.**
- Written paper: Question 1 is compulsory (15 short parts, answer 12, about 36% of the paper), then core long questions. Section B covers your two options (about 40% of the paper).
- 2027 brief (independent living for an ageing population, electro-mechanical, max 500 mm) is as you described ⚠.

**Coursework – how to score highly.** For each piece the app has marking criteria, common mistakes and what top-grade work looks like, all written in my own words. Two points specific to you:
- **Physics:** in a sealed, opaque box the light is also absorbed and becomes heat. Think about how light leaves the box, and about a calibration run with a known-power heater, while you revise your method.
- **Technology:** a Pi 5 plus motors on a single USB-C supply can brown out when the motors start. Budget the current and set the A4988 current limit.

## 4. How time is split

Weight per subject = **0.4 + (grade gap × share of marks still in the written exam)**, plus 0.6 for new Physics content. Irish is ×0.6 because it isn't counted for points.

Within a subject, time is split across sections **by the marks each carries** (the `mk` values in the data). For example, English is P1 comprehension 100 · composition 100 · Othello 60 · comparative 70 · poetry 70, so no area gets crowded out. Topics inside a section are ranked by **how often examined × difficulty × your confidence × spaced-repetition due date**, and poems rotate so each of your five poets gets at least six.

Coursework milestones get their blocks first, spread evenly before each due date.

Share of exam-study blocks (coursework comes on top of these):

| Period | English | Maths | Physics | Geog | DCG | Irish | Tech |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| October | 33% | 21% (+ grind) | 19% | 11% | 9% | 5% | 2% |
| Mid-January | 25% | 18% | 16% | 9% | 7% | 22% | 2% |
| Mid-March (oral) | 21% | 15% | 13% | 7% | 6% | 36% | 1% |
| April onward | 29% | 20% | 21% | 10% | 9% | 8% | 3% |

From 5 April most blocks become timed SEC past-paper sections, marked with the marking scheme. During the exams, each evening goes to the next paper. The mocks don't change anything, as you asked.

**Exam frequency.** The "how often examined" labels are my estimates from the pattern of recent SEC papers (2015–2026), not a formal count – the SEC archive couldn't be opened from the build environment. Past-paper practice is mapped by paper, section and question type, and the app has a per-subject past-papers tracker.
